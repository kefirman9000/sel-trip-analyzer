**The big one: a History tab.** Load relay files from many stations and dates and see, per location, what keeps happening, what's getting worse and where to look first. This release also includes everything built since v1.2.0 (the 2.0 work was never released on its own).

### New: History (fourth tab, `Alt+4`)
- **Add to history** from the Events page, or tick *Add new files automatically*. Files and events are kept in the browser; an event read from a .CEV, a .SUM and a .HIS counts once.
- **Ranked problem locations** with a score you can open up, **recurring issues** (faults 3+ times in 90 days, anything else 3+ times in a year), **fault hot spots** at the same distance on a line, and **increasing / decreasing trends** (last 90 days and year on year).
- **Breaker health**: clearing time over time, flagged when it's getting slower, before it's actually slow.
- **Weather** at the hour of every trip: storm or calm, and which recurring faults happen in calm weather.
- **Customer impact**: enter customers per circuit for customer interruptions and SAIFI / MAIFI estimates.
- **PRC-004 reviews and notes are shared** between the History and the Events page; misoperations and overdue reviews per location.
- **What changed** since the last import, a **work log** marked on the charts, and **watch a folder** (Chrome / Edge).
- **Separate histories** (by region, team…), **combine** them, and **export all histories as one file**.
- **Exports**: Word report with charts, PDF, text, events and locations CSV, and a **read-only snapshot** (one .html) to send to someone.
- Click any event to see the evidence, step through a recurring issue, and open its waveforms.
- Also available on its own: `SEL_Trip_History.html` (same screen).

### Since v1.2.0
- Three pages: **Events**, **Summary** and **Waveforms** (graphs, phasors, harmonics, every relay element, 50/51 checked against the settings, notes pinned on the graph).
- **Operation review (NERC PRC-004)** for every trip, with due dates and a review log export.
- **Why?** on each event: the evidence behind the conclusion, linked to the graph.
- Trip summaries from **any maker's COMTRADE**; reads **.zip** files and **settings saved as text**.
- **Weather** at each event (Stations & weather), **notes** on every event, **watch a folder**.
- **Export options**: choose sections, pictures, paper and CSV columns; files named after the relay and dates.

Files: `SEL_Trip_Analyzer.exe` (Windows), `SEL_Trip_Analyzer.html` (any browser), `SEL_Trip_History.html`, and two sample sets.
