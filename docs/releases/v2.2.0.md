**Plain English switch.** Turn on *Plain English* (top right, next to Dark mode) and everything is worded for people who don't read relay codes.

- `AG T · INST ZONE1 A G` becomes *"Tripped: phase A touched ground about 42% of the way along the line; Zone 1 distance protection opened the breaker."*
- **Events table**: plain event types ("A to ground", "B and C", "No fault found") and a full sentence for what happened.
- **Summary**: the quick summary in sentences and a *Plain English* line at the top of each event's details; Word, PDF and text exports follow.
- **History tab**: everyday names for issues ("Recloser gave up and the circuit stayed off"), plain event details (what it was, where, what tripped it, how long the breaker took in seconds), the relay's sequence explained, "outages per customer" for SAIFI.
- **History reports, CSV and snapshots** follow the switch; a snapshot opens the way it was made. The events CSV always has a plain-English column.
- The relay's codes stay in the details and on hover, and the analysis (*Probable cause*, *CHECK* lines) keeps its technical wording.
- The switch is remembered and shared by the Analyzer and the History page.
